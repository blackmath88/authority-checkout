Prototype 04 · live authority drift

When does the checkout stop being true?

Prototype 01.1 proved the mechanics of one artifact serving two consumers. Prototype 04 adds time: upstream authority, classification and delegation can change after compilation. The checkout remains derived and must never become a stale second source of truth.

Invariant AD-01: no runtime decision may rely on stale checkout state when that field is declared live or invalidating.
