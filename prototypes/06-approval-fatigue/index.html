What this tests
not a UX benchmark
The experiment does not claim to solve approval fatigue. It asks whether Authority Checkout is relying on a human gate as if it were deterministic. If reviewers start approving prohibited effects under repetition, the architecture must move more decisions into deterministic policy and reserve human review for genuine ambiguity.
