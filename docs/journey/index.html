Authority Checkout / learning journal

What I thought. What broke. What changed.

This is not a polished theory. It is a chronological record of an idea being challenged: wrong assumptions, missing architectural knowledge, reframings, and the questions that remain open.

The journal is intentionally modular. The base entries live in entries.js; later sparring sessions append their own files. Critiques are inputs, not truth, so newer entries may include provenance and verification state.
Authority Checkout is an exploratory research prototype. A failed assumption is useful evidence.
